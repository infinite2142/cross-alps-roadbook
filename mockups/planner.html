288 km distance ≈6h 30m moving (5h 45m – 7h 15m) 5 passes 2,757 m high point

Two new things here. The moving time carries its spread, because the router is out by up to a third on hairpin roads and the error reverses on motorway — a single figure would be a confident lie. And the daylight line is free: sunrise and sunset are already computed locally for any date and place, and in October the margin goes negative before the driving does.

Pass chips for reference, in all four states: usually open borderline usually shut through route — the dashed border means a weaker claim: no dated record, medium confidence, or not checked in a year.
