Book before you fly

Ordered by how badly it goes wrong if you skip it.

    Rules that will catch you out

    Passo Gardena is open

    Several blogs say it closes to all traffic this autumn. It doesn't. The restriction was reduced to a digital simulation with no physical barriers and no penalties; the province has confirmed no restriction. The real ZTL is targeted at a later season.

    Night driving bans

    Grossglockner runs 06:00–19:30 on the autumn timetable, last entry 18:45. Timmelsjoch is 07:00–20:00. Both are enforced with gates, not signs. Build the day backwards from the gate time.

    Austrian vignette

    German-plated rental cars usually don't carry one. Buy the 10-day digital vignette (€12.80) at a petrol station or ADAC office for immediate validity. Fine is €200+.

    Parking colours in Italy

    White is free but may need a disc, blue is paid, yellow is residents only and gets towed in Ortisei and Cortina. On the passes, park only in marked gravel bays.

    Places not to improvise

    Lago di Braies can't be driven to 09:00–16:00 without a reservation. Alpe di Siusi is closed to private traffic 09:00–17:00. Neither is on these routes.

    Check the morning before

    alpen-paesse.ch for Swiss and border passes, suedtirol.com/verkehr for South Tyrol, grossglockner.at webcams for the Glockner. Roadworks near Umbrail have run alternating closures — check before committing.
